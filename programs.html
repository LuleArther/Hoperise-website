OUR MISSION IN ACTION

Empowering Through Action

Our programs are designed to create lasting change by addressing the interconnected needs of the communities we serve. From education and health to economic empowerment, we provide a holistic support system.

medical_services Healthcare & Sanitation

Healthcare Access & Community Sanitation (HACS)

Through Valentine Medical Centre, we provide testing for malaria, typhoid, TB, and brucella, maternal care, minor surgery, and treatment for mild to moderate conditions for vulnerable children and adults.

We also support families and children living with HIV/AIDS through adherence counselling and referrals back into treatment, while outreach teams bring check-ups, immunisation, family planning, nutritional support, and other essential services closer to underserved households.

With Village Health Teams, we deliver WASH, hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention education to strengthen household-level health outcomes.

700+ Medical Services 45+ Homes Reached
school Education & Mentorship

EduSave — Education Sponsorship & Mentorship

EduSave identifies children living with HIV who are out of school, have dropped out, or are at risk of dropping out, then supports their return through school fees and scholastic materials.

In partnership with EduSenga and local schools, we provide psychosocial support and mentorship on hygiene, life goals, leadership, mental health, and girl-child empowerment.

We also run menstrual hygiene, reusable pad-making, and reproductive health awareness sessions while nurturing student-led “Each One, Support One” champion groups.

90+ Children Supported 100% Retention Rate
work Skills & Livelihoods

SESLD — Self-Help Entrepreneurial Skilling & Livelihoods Development

SESLD equips young people, women, and vulnerable families with practical skills such as carpentry, tailoring, hairdressing, and craft shoe making to create immediate income opportunities.

Beyond vocational training, we build entrepreneurship and financial literacy so participants can start, grow, and sustain dignified livelihoods away from risky and unsustainable survival activities.

50+ Youth Trained 15+ Businesses Started
eco Climate Adaptation

EcoAdapt — Climate Adaptation & Environmental Sustainability

EcoAdapt establishes agroforestry home gardens, trains families in organic farming, and promotes biogas and recycling initiatives that reduce waste while creating income opportunities.

5 Household Gardens 3 School Gardens

Our Holistic Model

Our programs are not isolated silos. They function like an ecosystem—where health supports learning, learning builds skills, and skills drive environmental stewardship. This holistic approach ensures that the impact we make today grows and sustains itself for generations.
