Our History

From a Single Act of Compassion to a Community of Hope.

HopeRise, initially the ‘Hope for a Child’s Sanctuary’ (HACS) program, was founded in 2019 by a compassionate and dedicated social worker out of a deep-seated desire to address the pressing needs of vulnerable children and families in the community.

2019

The Seed is Planted

What began as the ‘Hope for a Child’s Sanctuary’ (HACS) program started with supporting just one household, providing essential support to a family of seven. This single act of compassion laid the foundation for our future growth.

eco

Founding Vision

The initial vision was simple: to intervene where the need was most acute. It was about restoring dignity and offering a glimmer of hope, one family at a time, ensuring that vulnerable children had the chance to thrive.

2021

A Network of Compassion

By 2021, our efforts had expanded significantly. We were serving over 45 homes and more than 90 children. This period marked the launch of our "EduSave" program, broadening our impact from basic needs to educational empowerment.

Today

Holistic Development

HopeRise has evolved into a comprehensive support system, offering not just aid but also vocational training, mentorship, and community-based programs aimed at fostering long-term self-sufficiency.

park
CURRENT MILESTONE

Empowering Futures

We are redefining resilience by creating a multi-faceted ecosystem that provides education, vocational training, and mental health support, empowering individuals to build a brighter future for themselves and their communities.
