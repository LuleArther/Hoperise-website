Our Leadership

A Message from the Founder

"Hope is not a passive state, but a dynamic force we build together."

For over a decade, HopeRise has been more than an organization; it’s been a sanctuary. We started with a simple belief: that no person living with HIV/AIDS should walk their journey alone, and no child should lose their future to poverty.

Our work in Uganda is about weaving resilience into the daily fabric of our communities. Every donation is a thread, and every volunteer is a pair of hands helping us create a tapestry of sustainable growth and dignity.

Mukasa Dennis

Founder, Hoperise
