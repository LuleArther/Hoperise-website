About Us

A community of hope in action.

HopeRise began in 2019 as the Hope for a Child’s Sanctuary (HACS), supporting just one household in Kirangira. Today, we work with families, schools, and health partners to deliver healthcare, education sponsorship, livelihood skills, and environmental resilience that restore dignity and opportunity for every generation.

50+ Youth Trained
