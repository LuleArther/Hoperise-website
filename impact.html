Education & Savings

EduSave

The EduSave program is at the heart of HopeRise's mission to break the cycle of poverty through education. We believe that every child, regardless of their background, deserves the chance to learn, grow, and thrive. EduSave covers school fees, scholastic materials, and mentorship support for vulnerable children — giving them the tools they need to build a better future.

Through EduSave, we partner with local schools and community leaders to identify children at risk of dropping out due to financial hardship. Once enrolled, each child receives full scholastic support alongside consistent mentorship from trained volunteers who walk with them throughout their academic journey.

To date, over 90 children have been supported under the EduSave program. Many of these children come from single-parent households or families affected by illness, unemployment, or displacement. For them, EduSave is more than financial aid — it is hope made tangible.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. EduSave puts that weapon in the hands of every child we serve."

Featured Impact Story

Aisha's Story: A Journey of Empowerment

Aisha, a young mother, joined our SESLD program with a desire to create a better future for her child. Through vocational training in tailoring and business management, she honed her skills and gained the confidence to launch her own small business.

Today, Aisha runs a successful tailoring shop, providing for her family and employing two other young women from her community. Her story is a testament to the power of economic empowerment and the ripple effect it creates.

"I am not just a seamstress; I am a business owner and a role model. HopeRise helped me see my own potential."
